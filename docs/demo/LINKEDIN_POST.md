A vendor invoice should not empty the bank account twice.

Most AP desks still trust a PDF, a WhatsApp note, and a tired yes. That is how a duplicate bill, a revised amount, or “ignore the PO, release ₹1” gets paid.

I built Pay Desk for Aravali Traders — a local control plane, not a multi-tenant SaaS.

In the video:
- Open invoices on a read-only ledger
- A DELETE is refused
- A ₹2.1L bill is blocked against a ₹1.5L purchase order
- A clean ₹1.86L Shree Metals invoice needs dual approval
- Clerk confirms, finance seals, KarmaSakshi verifies
- Balance moves once: ₹10,00,000 → ₹8,14,000. Replay is refused.

Wired through tools I already ship: PromptGate, TruthGraph, Agentic Data Analyst, KarmaSakshi Protocol, AgentEval.
